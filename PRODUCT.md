# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- Fellow researchers and technical readers who find the site via publications or professional links — they're verifying credibility, but stay for the person
- Adventurers, readers, and newsletter subscribers from Full Stack Human who connect with the personal writing first
- Recruiters or collaborators who want to understand Avinesh as a whole person, not just a résumé

## Product Purpose

A personal site that represents the whole person — not just the researcher at Apple, but the traveler, adventurer, writer, and human-being-in-progress. The I'm Possible List is as central to the identity as the publications list. Success looks like: a visitor leaves knowing what Avinesh stands for, not just where he works.

## Brand Personality

Curious, disciplined, alive. The explorer leads — research gives it weight, adventure gives it pulse. Neither swallows the other.

## Anti-references

- **Generic academic page**: plain white, credentials front-and-center, publications list as personality, no sense of who lives behind the CV. The site should never feel like it could belong to any researcher.
- **Hustle-culture blog**: loud growth-hacking energy, self-promotional tone, listicles and optimization content.
- **Safe corporate portfolio**: polished but forgettable. Could belong to anyone in tech. Zero edge.

## Design Principles

1. **The explorer leads** — Open with curiosity and lived experience; credentials are context, not the headline. Research depth and human breadth aren't in tension — both are the point.
2. **Both things are true** — A researcher who paraglides, does Vipassana, and tracks Spartan races isn't a contradiction. The site holds these without compartmentalizing them.
3. **Purple is a commitment** — The brand color (#b509ac) is intentional and stays. Don't neutralize it into gray or white. Let it be present.
4. **Specific over generic** — Real stories, real stakes, actual experiences. No abstraction, no corporate smooth. The I'm Possible List is more interesting than a mission statement.
5. **Discovery over declaration** — Motion, reveals, and transitions should feel like finding something, not watching a slideshow. Content should invite rather than announce.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text contrast, 3:1 large text)
- Dark mode supported and intentional — not an afterthought
- Reduced motion support for all animations
